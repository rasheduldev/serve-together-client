
# ServeTogether

**Live Site URL:** [https://servetogether.vercel.app](https://servetogether.vercel.app)

---

## About ServeTogether

ServeTogether is a community-driven social development event management platform designed to connect passionate volunteers with local causes, charity drives, and community service initiatives. Whether it's winter clothing distributions, neighborhood clean-ups, or tree plantation drives, ServeTogether makes organizing and joining local impact effortless.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Community Event Management:** Authenticated users can create, update, and delete their own local social development drives with future-date validation.

- **Interactive Discovery & Filtering:** Browse upcoming community initiatives through a responsive grid layout equipped with category filter tabs and keyword search.

- **Seamless Event Participation:** Public visitors can view comprehensive event details, while logged-in volunteers can join drives instantly with one click and track their commitments.

- **Authentication & Protected Routes:** Email/password and social login mechanisms with protected routes for private pages, including Create, Manage, and Joined Events.

- **Dynamic Theme Customization:** Switch between light and dark modes for a comfortable browsing experience across different devices and environments.
